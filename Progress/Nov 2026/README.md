# Graphyti: Progress Update Demo

▶️ **Watch:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1riq26VTZd8](https://youtu.be/zu5ixjfP5lU)

Graphyti is a procedural 3D world engine for generating synthetic training data.
Worlds are built algorithmically, so they are reproducible and controllable with
no black box. AI is used only as a design co-pilot that turns prompts into
generator parameters.

## What's in this demo
- **Procedural scenes**: urban (residential / commercial / neon, plus a toggleable beach zone) and forest, assembled from modular parts
- **AI design co-pilot**: text prompts to regenerate city areas, segment objects, switch camera views, and trigger captures
- **Auto dataset capture**: POV or satellite camera, auto-traverse or manual WASD
  - Outputs: RGB, segmentation masks, bounding boxes
  - Frames can be stitched to video
