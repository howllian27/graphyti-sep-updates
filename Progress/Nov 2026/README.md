# Graphyti: Progress Update Demo

▶️ **Watch:** [https://youtu.be/zu5ixjfP5lU](https://youtu.be/zu5ixjfP5lU)

NEW WEBSITE AVAILABLE: https://graphyti.app

Graphyti is a procedural 3D world engine for generating synthetic training data.
Worlds are built algorithmically, so they are reproducible and controllable with
no black box. AI is used only as a design co-pilot that turns prompts into
generator parameters.

## What's in this demo
- **Advanced scene planner**: Interiors, cities and forests laid out from a prompt: rooms that make sense, streets that connect, terrain, rivers and lakes that sit in their basins.
- **Parametric articulated assets**: Cars, buses, trucks, aircraft, trains, boats and mechs generated from parameters — with real hinges, wheels and rotors that move.
- **Browser based vfx**: Lakes and rivers as data, simulated fire, volumetric haze, rain and wet streets, black holes, portals, shields and holograms, and every lamp a real light.
